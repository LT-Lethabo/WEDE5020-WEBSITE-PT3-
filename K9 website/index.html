Welcome to Lethabo's K9 Academia of Protection

About

K9 units, made up of trained dogs and their handlers, are crucial in maintaining security and safety across various environments. The dogs’ unique abilities, such as a heightened sense of smell, agility, and obedience, make them the best weapons. Lethabo's K9 Academia of Protection provides fully trained personal protection dogs and dog training to families, farmers, game farms, business owners, and security companies, providing you with the best weapon by your side.

Our Training programs include:

  • Search and Rescue
  • Service and Guidance
  • Close Protection
  • Obedience
  • Patrol and Apprehension

Why Choose Us

At Lethabo's K9 Academia, we don't just train dogs; we forge elite, reliable protectors. Our K9 units undergo real-world scenario testing to ensure absolute reliability, split-second obedience, and unwavering loyalty when it matters most.
